Figure X. Sensitivity of the MIS 6 conditional predictive-information (PI) analysis to event-age uncertainty. Histograms summarize fits to 10,000 accepted A+ age realizations; pale shading spans the 2.5th–97.5th percentiles, and red lines show results for the original extracted event sequence. (a) In-sample nested Poisson log-likelihood gain expressed as bits per event. (b) Nominal asymptotic likelihood-ratio-test p value; the dashed line marks p = 0.05. (c) ΔAICc for the phase-augmented model minus the reduced model; the dashed zero line separates preference for the full (negative) and reduced (positive) models. Percentages in (b) and (c) are the fractions of age realizations satisfying the indicated criterion and are robustness proportions, not Monte Carlo-calibrated p values. (d) Preferred precession phase, unwrapped within ±180° of the original estimate; 0° denotes a precession-index minimum. Every realization uses the same 0.2-Kyr bins, complete 5-Kyr event-history support, LR04 and CO₂ covariates, and sine/cosine precession terms as the point-age MIS 6 analysis. Of the realizations, 9,938 contribute 20 response events and 62 contribute 19 because one event crosses the 191.5 Kyr BP complete-history boundary; multiple events in a bin are retained as counts. No Rayleigh test or proxy-resolution covariate is included.
