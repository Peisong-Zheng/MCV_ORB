Figure X. Monte Carlo uncertainty of the provisional 21-event MIS 6 composite chronology. (a) Marginal sampled-age offsets from the original maximum-gradient event picks, whose absolute ages are listed beside the event labels. Points mark Monte Carlo medians; thick and thin lines show the 16th–84th and 2.5th–97.5th percentile ranges, respectively; the dashed zero line represents the original extracted event sequence. Colors identify the MF, Huagapo, and Sofular source records. (b) Pearson correlations among sampled event-age anomalies. The block structure reflects the shared chronology draw applied within each source record; white boundaries separate contiguous source-record sections in composite-event order. Results summarize 10,000 accepted A+ realizations combining the nine equally weighted event-definition settings with record-specific chronology uncertainty, conditional on preservation of the fixed event order. Cross-record synchronization uncertainty and proxy data resolution are not included. Ages are in Kyr BP, with BP referenced to 1950 CE.
