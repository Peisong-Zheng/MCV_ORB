# Coastline used by the conceptual mechanism figure

`ne_110m_land.geojson` is the Natural Earth 1:110 million land layer, release
v5.1.2, downloaded on 2026-09-21 from:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

Natural Earth data are public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The modern coastline supplies geographic orientation only. Colored ocean areas,
sea ice, and arrows in `Figure_orbital_mechanism.py` are conceptual annotations,
not reconstructed glacial boundaries, measured fields, or model output.
The local copy makes the figure reproducible without network access or Cartopy.
