Figure X. Provisional MIS 6 composite event chronology derived from three speleothem records. (a) Age-ordered composite sequence and the source record assigned to each event; the dashed line at 175 Kyr BP marks the transition from the MF record to the later Huagapo and Sofular records. (b) MF δ¹⁸O record and event labels MIS 6.1–6.16 from Fohlmeister et al. (2023). (c) Huagapo δ¹⁸O record from Burns et al. (2019); Held et al. (2024) source labels 6.9, 6.12, and 6.13 are shown in square brackets and renumbered MIS 6.17, 6.20, and 6.21 in the composite. (d) Sofular δ¹³C record and event labels from Held et al. (2024), with Held events 6.10 and 6.11 renumbered MIS 6.18 and MIS 6.19. Gray points and colored lines show the observations and Gaussian-smoothed records, respectively. Filled colored circles mark provisional event ages defined by the expected-direction maximum proxy gradient within ±400 yr of the literature-based search positions, after gap-bounded 1-yr interpolation and Gaussian smoothing (σ = 50 yr for MF and Sofular; σ = 100 yr for Huagapo); thin leaders connect event labels to these estimates. Gray open squares and horizontal bars near the x-axis in (c) and (d) show individual-stalagmite U–Th age controls and their reported 2σ errors, not event-age intervals. Huagapo controls directly bracket the selected P10-H2 events; the Sofular controls belong to the So-4 and So-57 components of the published iscam stack, and three additional endpoints completing those brackets lie outside the enlarged panel. The Sofular proxy axis is reversed. Ages are in Kyr BP, with BP referenced to 1950 CE.
